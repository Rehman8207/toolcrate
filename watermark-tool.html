Frequently asked questions

Is there a page limit for PDF watermarking?

No. Every page of every PDF you add is watermarked — there's no artificial page cap, whether your document has 5 pages or 500.

Are my files uploaded to a server?

No. Watermarking happens locally in your browser using JavaScript. Your images and PDFs never leave your device.

Can I watermark a whole folder of images at once?

Yes. Use "Choose folder" to select every image and PDF inside a folder, or "Choose files" to multi-select individually. Everything is processed and packaged into one ZIP download.

Can I use a logo instead of text?

Yes. Upload a PNG, JPG or WebP logo — it can be applied alongside or instead of a text watermark, with its own position, size and opacity.

What file formats are supported?

JPG, PNG and WebP images, and PDF documents. You can mix formats in a single batch.

Is this really free?

Yes — no account, no watermark on the watermark, no page or file-count limits enforced by us.
