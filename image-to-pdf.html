Free Image to PDF Converter — No Upload, No Limit

ToolCrate's Image to PDF tool converts your JPG, PNG, and WebP images into a single, downloadable PDF document entirely inside your browser. No files are ever sent to a server — everything runs locally using the jsPDF JavaScript library.

How to Convert Images to PDF

  1. Click the upload area or drag and drop your images
  2. Preview the images — they appear in order as PDF pages
  3. Click Download PDF to save the merged document

Frequently Asked Questions

Are my images uploaded to a server?

No. The conversion uses the jsPDF library running entirely in your browser. Your images never leave your device and are never sent to any server or third party.

Is there a page or file size limit?

There is no artificial page cap. You can convert as many images as you need. Very large batches may take a few seconds to process depending on your device.

What image formats are supported?

JPG, JPEG, PNG, and WebP are all supported. Each image is placed on its own page in the PDF, scaled to fill the full page width.

Can I reorder the images before converting?

Images are added in the order you select them. To control the order, select files in the desired sequence or rename them with numbered prefixes (01_, 02_, etc.) before selecting.
