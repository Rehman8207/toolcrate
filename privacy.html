Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information ToolCrate collects and how it is used. ToolCrate is designed around a simple principle: your content stays on your device.

What we do NOT collect

  • Anything you type, paste, or upload into a tool (text, images, JSON, passwords, dates, prices) is processed entirely in your browser using JavaScript. It is never uploaded to, or stored on, any ToolCrate server.
  • We do not require accounts, so we do not collect names, emails, or passwords to use any tool.

What we may collect

  • Local storage: your light/dark theme preference is saved in your browser's local storage (key toolcrate-theme) so it persists between visits. This stays on your device and is never transmitted.
  • Basic analytics/hosting logs: our hosting provider (Netlify) may log standard technical data such as IP address, browser type, and pages visited, used only in aggregate to understand traffic and keep the site running.
  • Advertising: if ToolCrate displays ads through Google AdSense, Google may use cookies to serve relevant ads. You can learn more and opt out of personalized advertising at google.com/settings/ads.

Third-party services

ToolCrate loads fonts from Google Fonts and, on the QR code tool, a QR-generation library from a public CDN (cdnjs). These services may see the fact that your browser requested a file, per their own standard server logs, but do not receive the content you enter into ToolCrate's tools.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the update will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date.

Contact
