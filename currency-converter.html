Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Where do the exchange rates come from?

We use the free open.er-api.com exchange rate API, which updates rates daily. Rates are indicative and may differ slightly from what banks or payment processors offer — always check with your provider for the exact rate before making large transactions.

Is there a limit on how many conversions I can do?

No — convert as many amounts as you want, in any currency, as often as you like. No limits, no rate limiting, no signup.

Can I use this offline?

Once the page has loaded the rates, you can keep converting even if your internet drops briefly. Refresh the page to fetch the latest rates again.

Is my data stored anywhere?

No. The amounts you enter never leave your browser. Everything is processed client-side using JavaScript.
