About ToolCrate

Last updated: 2026

ToolCrate is a small, free collection of everyday tools — a word counter, case converter, unit converter, QR code generator, password generator, image compressor, JSON formatter, age calculator, and a couple of calculators students and shoppers use often.

The idea behind it is simple: most "free" tool sites online make you wait through ads, create an account, or upload your file to a server before you can get anything done. ToolCrate doesn't do any of that. Every tool runs entirely as JavaScript in your own browser — nothing you type or upload is ever sent anywhere.

Why we built it

We wanted a single place with the small utilities people reach for constantly — counting words, converting units, generating a QR code — without friction. No login walls, no artificial file-size limits designed to push you toward a paid plan.

How it's funded

ToolCrate is currently free with no ads. As the site grows we may run limited, clearly-labelled advertising through Google AdSense to keep it free and cover hosting costs — we'll never sell or share what you type into a tool.
