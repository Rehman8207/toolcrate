About ToolCrate

Last updated: 2026

ToolCrate is a free collection of everyday tools — word counter, case converter, unit converter, QR code generator, password generator, image compressor, JSON formatter, age calculator, CGPA calculator, currency converter, PDF tools, watermark tool and more.

Most "free" tool sites make you wait through ads, create an account, or upload your file to a server. ToolCrate doesn't. Every tool runs entirely as JavaScript in your browser — nothing you type or upload is ever sent anywhere.

Why we built it

One place with the small utilities people reach for constantly — without friction. No login walls, no file-size limits, no PDF page caps.

How it's funded

ToolCrate is free with no ads. As the site grows we may run clearly-labelled advertising to cover hosting costs — we'll never sell or share what you type into a tool.
