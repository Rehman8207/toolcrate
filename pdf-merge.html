Free PDF Merger — No Upload, No Limit

ToolCrate's PDF Merger combines your PDF documents entirely inside your browser. No files are ever sent to a server — everything runs locally using the pdf-lib JavaScript library.

How to Merge PDFs

  1. Click the upload area or drag and drop your PDFs
  2. Add as many files as you like — they will merge in the order listed
  3. Click Download Merged PDF to save the document

Frequently Asked Questions

Are my PDFs uploaded to a server?

No. The merging uses a local library running entirely in your browser. Your PDFs never leave your device and are never sent to any server or third party.

Is there a file size limit?

There is no artificial limit. You can merge as many PDFs as you need. Very large batches may take a few seconds to process depending on your device's memory.

Can I reorder the PDFs before merging?

Currently, files are added in the order you select them. You can remove individual files from the list or clear all to restart.
