Free PDF Merger — Page-Level Visual Reordering & Control

ToolCrate's PDF Merger gives you complete visual control over your documents. Merge multiple PDFs into one unified file, reorder pages intuitively with drag-and-drop, rotate pages by 90 degrees, and remove unneeded pages — all inside your browser. Your files never leave your computer, ensuring 100% privacy and security.

How to Merge & Organize PDFs

  1. Upload your PDFs: Drag and drop single files, batches, or an entire folder into the drop zone.
  2. Visual Page Control: Every page is rendered as an interactive thumbnail. Drag cards across the grid to place pages in any order.
  3. Rotate or Delete: Use the 🔄 rotate button in the card's top-left corner to turn pages 90° clockwise, or click the 🗑️ delete button to remove pages.
  4. Preview & Merge: Check the horizontal live preview strip to verify the exact final document sequence, then click Merge PDFs to download.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are my PDF documents uploaded to a server?

Never. The entire workflow — parsing, rendering thumbnails, reordering pages, rotating, and generating the final PDF — runs 100% client-side inside your browser using the open-source pdf-lib and pdf.js engines. No telemetry or file uploads occur.

Can I combine pages from different PDF files in any order?

Yes. Once you add multiple PDFs, all pages appear together in a single draggable grid. You can move any page from document A ahead of or between pages of document B seamlessly.

Can I upload a folder containing multiple PDFs?

Yes. Use the "Select Folder" button or drag a folder directly onto the drop area. ToolCrate scans and imports all valid PDF documents found inside.

Is there a limit on file size or number of pages?

There are no artificial software limits, paywalls, or watermarks. The only boundary is your computer or mobile device's available browser memory.
