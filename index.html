Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about ToolCrate and how our free browser tools work.

Are these tools really free?

Yes. All 18+ tools on ToolCrate are 100% free forever. No signup, no hidden charges, no premium plan.

Do you upload my files to a server?

No. Every tool runs entirely in your browser using JavaScript. Your files, text, and images never leave your device. You can even disconnect from the internet after the page loads — everything still works.

Is there a file size or page limit?

No artificial limits. Upload folders, multiple files at once, and PDFs of any length. The only constraint is your device's available memory.

Do I need to create an account to use these tools?

No account required. Open any tool and start using it immediately. No email, no signup, no waiting.

Which tools are most popular?

The most used tools are Image Compressor, PDF Merger, Word Counter, QR Code Generator, Image to PDF, and CGPA Calculator. Students, freelancers, and developers use them daily.

Can I use these tools on my phone?

Yes. All tools are fully responsive and work on mobile, tablet, and desktop. The interface adapts automatically to your screen size.

Are these tools safe for sensitive documents?

Absolutely. Since nothing is uploaded to any server, your files stay on your device from start to finish. This makes ToolCrate ideal for passports, CNICs, contracts, and other sensitive documents.

Do the tools work offline?

Yes — after the page loads, most tools work without an internet connection. Some tools like the Currency Converter need internet only for fetching live rates.

How do I use the PDF Merger?

Open the PDF Merger tool, drag your PDF files in (or select a folder), drag-and-drop pages to reorder, rotate or delete pages as needed, then click Merge to download your combined PDF. Everything runs in your browser — no uploads, no page limits.

Is my text or data stored anywhere?

No. Your data lives only in your browser's memory while you use a tool. Close the tab and everything is gone. Nothing is stored on any server.
