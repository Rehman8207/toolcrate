What Makes a Password Unhackable?

  • Length: Aim for at least 14 to 16 characters long.
  • Character Variety: Mix uppercase letters, lowercase letters, digits, and special symbols (!@#$%).
  • Uniqueness: Never reuse the same password across multiple online platforms or emails.
