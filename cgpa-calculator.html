How to Calculate GPA and CGPA Step-by-step

Calculating your Grade Point Average (GPA) and Cumulative Grade Point Average (CGPA) is essential for monitoring academic standing on a standard 4.0 scale. GPA represents your average performance for a single semester, while CGPA measures your overall academic performance across all completed semesters.

How to Extract Semester GPA from Result CGPA

If your university result card shows your overall CGPA after a semester, use this simple mathematical formula to isolate your current semester GPA:

Semester GPA = [(New CGPA × Total Cumulative Credits) - (Previous CGPA × Previous Credits)] ÷ Current Semester Credits

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

What is the difference between GPA and CGPA?

GPA measures academic performance in a single term/semester, whereas CGPA calculates cumulative performance across all semesters combined.

How do I raise my CGPA in upcoming semesters?
