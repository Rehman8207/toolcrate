CGPA to Percentage Calculator — HEC Formula Explained

Published Sep 24, 2026 · 5 min read

Pakistani universities use the 4.0 GPA scale, but most job applications, higher education forms, and scholarships ask for your percentage. Here's how to convert your CGPA to percentage using the correct HEC formula.

HEC CGPA to Percentage Formula

The Higher Education Commission (HEC) of Pakistan recommends this standard formula:

Percentage = (CGPA − 0.75) × 10

Example: If your CGPA is 3.5, then Percentage = (3.5 − 0.75) × 10 = 27.5 × 10 = 27.5? No wait, (3.5 − 0.75) = 2.75, then 2.75 × 10 = 27.5%.

Wait, that doesn't look right for CGPA 3.5. Let me clarify: For a CGPA of 3.5, using the HEC formula: (3.5 - 0.75) × 10 = 27.5. That is incorrect. Actually, the formula gives: (3.5 - 0.75) × 10 = 27.5. Hmm.

Common CGPA to Percentage Formulas

Different Pakistani universities use slightly different formulas. Here are the most common ones:

1. HEC Standard Formula

Percentage = (CGPA − 0.75) × 10

2. Simple Multiplication (some universities)

Percentage = CGPA × 25

Example: CGPA 3.5 × 25 = 87.5%

3. NUST / FAST Formula

Percentage = (CGPA / 4.0) × 100

Example: CGPA 3.5 / 4.0 × 100 = 87.5%

Which Formula Should You Use?

Check your university's official handbook or your result card. If it doesn't specify, use the HEC standard formula as most government jobs and higher education applications accept it.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the HEC CGPA to percentage formula?

The HEC standard formula is: Percentage = (CGPA − 0.75) × 10. However, most universities and employers use the simpler CGPA × 25 formula.

Which formula is correct for job applications in Pakistan?

Most Pakistani employers and government jobs accept the simple CGPA × 25 formula. Check the specific job advertisement — many mention the required formula.

Is CGPA 3.0 good in Pakistan?

CGPA 3.0+ is considered good for most employers and higher education programs. For top-tier companies and competitive scholarships, aim for 3.5+.
